Remembering Our "Tivo"

Our hearts are shattered. Tyler James Feehan, our son, our "Tivo," was taken from us far too soon, alongside other American heroes lost or wounded in this same tragic attack.

In just 25 years, Tyler packed in more living than most people manage in a lifetime. He chased adventure relentlessly, whether it meant reaching Everest Base Camp or walking the Camino de Santiago across France and into Spain. As a leader, he never wavered in putting his soldiers first. He mentored them, shared his love of finance, and pushed the value of education every chance he got. He believed deeply in the power of military service, especially what it builds in a person: discipline, leadership, character. Everyone who knew him loved him.

Behind him, he leaves a family in mourning. His parents, his fiancée who was to become his wife upon his return from deployment, four sets of grandparents, and a wide circle of aunts, uncles, and cousins who adored him. Law school was next on his list, a goal he talked about with the same confidence he quoted from Legally Blonde: "What, like it's hard?"

To every man and woman who serves this country, our prayers remain with you, as they always have.

Tyler, we are proud of you beyond words. Devastated, yes, but prouder still. No parents could have asked for a better son, and carrying your legacy forward is now our purpose.

We ask only this: that in remembering Tyler, people pause to see the good this country still holds, rather than what divides it. To that end, we're building a scholarship in his name, one meant to open doors to education for those who serve or hope to. We're starting it with his death gratuity, and we welcome anyone moved to give.

— The Feehan Family

His Journey

Milestones

2019

Answered the Call

Born in Utah and raised in Georgia, Tyler enlisted in the Army National Guard while his father was stationed in Georgia — the beginning of his life of service. He later served with the 2nd Battalion, 222nd Field Artillery, Utah Army National Guard, while a student at Southern Utah University.

Spring 2024

Commissioned as an Officer

Earned his commission as an Air Defense Artillery officer through the SUU Army ROTC program — stepping into active-duty leadership.

2024–2026

Platoon Leader at Fort Bragg

Served with the 2nd Battalion, 55th Air Defense Artillery Regiment, 32nd Army Air and Missile Defense Command — leading and mentoring his soldiers.

July 18, 2026

Killed in Action

Fell while serving his country at Muwaffaq Salti Air Base in Jordan. Honored in a dignified transfer at Dover Air Force Base and remembered by a grateful nation.

Carry the Legacy Forward

The Tyler Feehan ROTC Scholarship

In Tyler's memory, his family has established a scholarship to support ROTC cadets — beginning at Southern Utah University, where his own journey as an officer began. Started with his military death gratuity, the fund carries forward everything he stood for: education, leadership, and service.

His parents ask that we honor him by choosing to "see the good this country still holds, rather than what divides it." Every gift helps a future officer answer the call.
